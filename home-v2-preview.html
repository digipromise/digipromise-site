FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does a DigiPromise AI agent actually do?

It replies to your customers on your website, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram in seconds, asks the questions your sales team would ask, books appointments or viewings, and sends the qualified lead to your team on WhatsApp, Telegram or email.

Is this a self-serve tool or do you set it up?

We set it up for you. Our team designs and trains the agent on your services, prices, FAQs and tone, connects your channels and tests it before launch. Setup starts within 24 hours of your kickoff call, and we agree the go-live date with you on that call.

Does it speak Arabic?

Yes. Every agent replies in Arabic, English and 100+ other languages, and switches automatically to the language your customer writes in.

Can I see what the agent says to my customers?

Yes. From the Growth plan up you get a client dashboard with every conversation, lead and booking, like the example above, and you can change the agent's answers any time.

How much does it cost?

Plans start from AED 2,500 a month. Every agent is custom-built for your business, so there is a one-time setup fee from AED 5,000. Pay annually and you save 15% on your plan, plus the setup fee is fully waived on Starter and 50% off on Growth, Scale and Enterprise.

What if it does not work for my business?

Every agent is designed around your business, your services and your customers. Before we start, we agree the exact scope with you, then we build and test it until it works the way you need. If you are not satisfied or change your mind, you can cancel any time and we refund the remaining amount under our refund policy. The setup fee covers the custom planning and build agreed with you, so it is not refundable. Monthly plans stay flexible: no lock-in, cancel any time, and come back within 6 months without paying setup again. We would rather earn your business every month.
